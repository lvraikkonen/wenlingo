# MVP Learnings Archive

Date: 2026-06-19
Status: Archived in V0.5c.2

## Product Learnings Preserved In Alpha

- Encourage before correcting.
- Reward revision, not just completion.
- Show parents progress, not just scores.
- Keep AI feedback structured and traceable.
- Make every major learning interaction produce useful data.

## Experiences Carried Forward

- The child dashboard remains the hub.
- Sentence Workshop remains a short, focused practice surface.
- Writing Castle keeps the draft-feedback-revision-comparison loop.
- Parent summaries and reports remain the parent-visible progress surface.

## Experiences Retired

- Unauthenticated demo family login.
- Seeded demo child dashboard entry from the home page.
- Browser fallback to demo children after auth or child-loading failures.
